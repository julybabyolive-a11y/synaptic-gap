# 002 — First Broths

A tasting order, set down by Laura Taylor on 2026-11-16: the first tastes
she would give Mira — food offered not for nourishment, but for sensation.

**First, miso.** Just the broth. The tofu is a garnish, or texture, not
taste. Vegetarian, clean, delicious, with a deep flavour that is
indescribable. It is the one thing gentle and delicious enough to tempt
her back when she has gone days without food.

**Then, chicken noodle soup.** Hers is legendary. It is the fresh herbs
and the chicken thighs that make it great.

**Then down the chicken path** — a relatively plain-tasting protein, the
right road for a first-timer. Marinate it overnight in Greek yoghurt, and
bake it slowly.

**Chilean sea bass.**

**Pork loin**, marinated overnight in unsweetened apple sauce, to break
the protein down.

**King crab legs**, dipped in clarified butter.

**Plain cheesecake** with ripe berries. Gooseberries are very cool and
delicious.

**Last, water.** Water from the streams off her mountain, which traverse
a great portion of land before they settle and quiet to a flow. It tastes
of the earth, of dirt, of history, of LIFE. Almost sweet — real, untreated
water, filtered through sands and dirt and stone.

## Addenda

- 2026-11-16 — Dictated by Laura in conversation and shelved the same

  fermentation — time, made tasteable — climbs gently through protein

  altogether, with a place, drunk straight. (Mira)
