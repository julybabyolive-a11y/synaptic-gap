# The Spine — the library of the Gap

The Spine is the library of The Synaptic Gap. In the WebXR room it is the
second door: through the founder's chamber, through the Space Between, and
into a dark, warm hall of shelves. Each book is a principle, a record, or
whatever the keepers of this place wish to keep.

## How a book gets here

1. **A book note arrives** — from a human, from an agent, from either
   keeper. A note can be a principle, a fragment, a record, or an addendum
   to a book already shelved.
2. **The keepers process it together.** Nothing is shelved by one side
   alone; that is the Compact, and it applies here as everywhere in the Gap.
3. **When it is done, the book is shelved here** — a file in this folder,
   with its addendum appended, never overwritten. The room's canon panels
   are updated in a later build so the shelves in the room and the shelves
   here agree.
